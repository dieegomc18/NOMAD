# NYC w Dad - Apps, Trackers, And Planning Notes

Last updated: 2026-04-11

These are useful items from the TikTok/place research that should not be treated as fixed map places yet. Use this as the "remember this later" sheet when building the final itinerary.

## Cherry Blossoms

- Best plan: keep Brooklyn Botanic Garden and Central Park as the two main cherry blossom options.
- Central Park has an official Spring Guide / bloom tracker from the Central Park Conservancy. Check it close to the travel dates because bloom timing changes quickly with weather.
- Brooklyn Botanic Garden is the stronger dedicated cherry blossom stop if the timing lines up, but it can be crowded around peak bloom.
- Central Park is easier to fold into a normal Manhattan day. Good candidates include Cherry Hill, the Reservoir area, and spots shown on the tracker.

Sources:
- Central Park Conservancy bloom tracker: https://www.centralparknyc.org/media/Blooms-in-Central-Park.json

## Chain / Multi-Location Places To Decide Later

- Blank Street Coffee: do not lock a location yet. Choose the best branch once the final day routes are set. Check the Blank Street app / offers at the time.
- Ichiran: there are multiple NYC locations. Pick based on the itinerary day, not now.
- Brooklyn Bagel & Coffee Company: keep as a flexible bagel option. Choose the branch near Union Square or the current route when the final plan is built.
- Playa Bowls, Joe & The Juice, Whole Foods, Shake Shack, Chipotle, Panda Express: keep as convenience / fallback options rather than destination stops unless one fits perfectly into a route.

## Apps Worth Installing Before The Trip

- Too Good To Go: worth installing for cheap surprise bags, especially bakeries, bagels, pizza, and Whole Foods. Best used opportunistically near where you already are, not as a reason to cross town. Pickup windows can be awkward.
- Slice: useful as a pizza-ordering backup, but not a planning priority. For famous slices like Scarr's, Joe's, Prince Street, or Village Square, it is often better to go directly unless the app shows a clear deal or shorter pickup.
- Happiest Hours NYC: worth installing if you want flexible happy-hour decisions by neighborhood/day/time. Use it while already in an area, especially LES, West Village, East Village, Williamsburg, or Midtown.
- Claim / Blank Street deals: only worth checking if you already planned to buy coffee nearby. Do not build the itinerary around it.

Sources:
- Too Good To Go official site: https://www.toogoodtogo.com/en-us
- Happiest Hours NYC: https://happiesthoursnyc.com/
- Blank Street Regulars help page: https://help.blankstreet.com/hc/en-us/articles/10111631517453-What-is-Blank-Street-Regulars

## Events / Time-Sensitive Plans

- JAPAN Fes: check the official 2026 New York schedule close to the trip dates. It is worth going if an event falls naturally near your route, but do not force it if it is far from the day plan.
- Earth Day on Governors Island: 2026 reports point to Saturday, April 18, with free programming. Worth combining with Staten Island Ferry / Lower Manhattan / waterfront planning only if the weather is good and the timing works.
- Central Park cherry blossom tracker: check the week of travel, because the best exact spots may change.

Sources:
- JAPAN Fes New York 2026: https://www.japanfes.com/newyork/2026
- Governors Island Earth Day event page: https://www.govisland.com/things-to-do/events/earth-day-on-governors-island
- 2026 Governors Island Earth Day report: https://thecitylife.org/2026/04/02/governors-island-to-host-fifth-annual-earth-day-celebration/

## Shopping / Outlet Decision

- Jersey Gardens: already in NOMAD and usually the more practical outlet choice for this trip. It is closer/easier from NYC than Woodbury and works better if the goal is value shopping without losing a whole day.
- Woodbury Common: better if designer outlet shopping is a major priority, but it is a larger time investment. Only choose it if shopping becomes a dedicated half/full-day plan.

## Food Strategy Notes

- Katz's budget alternative: keep Katz's on the list as the iconic option, but for cheaper Lower East Side / Chinatown food days, prioritize places already added like Shu Jiao Fu Zhou, Mei Lai Wah, Jin Mei Dumpling, 1915 Lanzhou, Xi'an Famous Foods, and Cheong Fun Cart.
- Eataly aperitivo / happy hour: treat as a timing-dependent option. Check current Eataly Flatiron or Downtown hours shortly before going; if nearby around aperitivo time, it can be a nice stop, but it should not be a fixed anchor until the final itinerary is set.
- Verlaine LES happy hour: good candidate if doing Lower East Side / East Village evening. Verify current happy-hour hours before committing.

## Final-Itinerary Reminders

- When the final day routes are built, revisit all chain/multi-location items and choose exact branches.
- Re-check event dates for JAPAN Fes and Governors Island Earth Day.
- Re-check cherry blossom status 1-3 days before the relevant park day.
- Use Too Good To Go, Happiest Hours, and Claim as opportunistic tools, not mandatory itinerary stops.
